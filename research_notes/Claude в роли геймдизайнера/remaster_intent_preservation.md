# Preserving design intent in remasters and remakes ("the same game, better made"): environment art, architecture, textures, mood, and the Gothic 1 Remake

Research date: 2026-10-21. Sources run from 2012 to June 2026. A note on reliability: items marked *(excerpt)* come from search-result excerpts of pages I could not open (403 errors, bot walls, redirects). Treat their exact wording as unverified. Everything else was checked against the fetched page.

## Q1. What do teams behind notable remakes and remasters say about preserving intent?

### Takeaway
Teams that were praised for fidelity locked the *structure*: layout, collision, encounters, proportions, silhouettes, the information on screen and the darkness. They spent the new budget on fidelity, meaning material detail, animation count, and resolving design detail that old hardware left ambiguous. They used the original's own references (concept art, surface types, original code and timing) as the target, not their own taste. Teams criticised for losing the soul usually wrote a brief whose aesthetic goals contradicted the original (Halo CE Anniversary's "clean and vibrant"), or chased "how people remember it" and drifted in palette (Oblivion Remastered).

### Cited Findings

**Demon's Souls (Bluepoint, 2020): 80.lv team interview, 22 June 2021**
- Art Director Mark Skelton: layout of level design, encounters and creatures "should not be adjusted"; the team saw that structure as the game's core. Focus went to "overall fidelity" and QoL. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- Lead Environment Artist Christian de los Santos: honour the original layout and gameplay; "the bounds of the collision were not altered". Layout and composition expansion was kept "to a minimum" while *detail* was expanded. Collision changes were mostly for new architecture and smooth camera/player movement, and Design and Production had to approve them. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- Lead Character Artist Bob Wallace: remade characters' proportions stayed close to the originals so combat felt familiar. Detail was added to designs that were "ambiguous in the original" because of polygon and texture limits. Elements of FromSoftware's 2008 concept art that the hardware could not show were incorporated. References: lore, hours of gameplay, the 2008 concept art, player videos and forums. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- Justin Wagner (materials): the team studied the original developer's intent, then reused the original *surface types* (cobblestone, mossy brick) when planning materials. Material direction was "raw, gritty, and detail-rich". Megascans were rarely used in the final game. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- Modular kits were built in four sub-theme tiers to show Boletaria's progression from mud and debris to an ornate royal centre. Composition of each world was set first to fix the tone. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- Animation went from about 4,000 to about 12,000 animations (facial animation, transitions) (Chris Torres). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- The team considered an Easy Mode and dropped it because it would change the balance. The number of worlds stayed the same despite fan requests for a sixth. Photo mode includes a "classic mode" filter evoking the PS3 look (Polygon, Oct 2020, as cited). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon%27s_Souls_(2020_video_game))
- Criticism: some critics felt the art update sacrificed part of the original's feel. A Vice Waypoint discussion said early Boletarian areas felt like portfolio work *(excerpt)*. VG247's review is titled "stylistically uneven" (cited on Wikipedia). — [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-demons-souls-remake-has-us-split); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon%27s_Souls_(2020_video_game))

**Diablo II: Resurrected (Vicarious Visions/Blizzard, 2021)**
- Rob Gallerani (Studio Design Director): "We are not iterating on the franchise"; "We have to keep the core of the game the same". The bug-fix rule was "What preserves the experience the players remember the most?" Exploits, duping and sync bugs were fixed, but beloved quirks were kept. Changes that could affect feel became optional (e.g. auto gold pickup). — [PlayStation LifeStyle, 21 Sep 2021](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- Information parity: "We never wanted you to have more or less information by toggling the graphics." Iconic characters, enemies and items keep the same silhouettes and visibility in both modes. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- Darkness is part of the design: "The original Diablo II is a very dark game". Global illumination was tuned to keep the same visual information. Example: a wooden club dropped in mud at night is nearly invisible, so extra lighting passes were needed. New physical grass hid items, so they built tech that pushes grass aside when loot drops. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- Dustin King (Associate Art Director): small mood embellishments (a candle) are allowed. If lighting reveals areas players should not see, "they had gone too far". He called the toggle "a blessing and a curse" because anyone can check pixel-for-pixel accuracy. QA ran side-by-side screenshot comparisons of what is visible in each mode. Alpha feedback from fans went down to the hue of items. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- The original game runs underneath at 25 fps with the new renderer on top. Cinematics were remade with the originals as a "frame-for-frame blueprint". Render framerate was decoupled per animation, so motion looks smoother but keeps the original timing. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/); [Blizzard Watch, 19/26 Feb 2021](https://blizzardwatch.com/2021/02/26/diablo-2-resurrected-remaster-feel/)
- "70/30 philosophy": keep at least 70% of the original look and colours while upgrading. The aim was to "look like Diablo 2 but updated — not a whole new game" (BlizzConline deep dive). — [Blizzard Watch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2021/02/26/diablo-2-resurrected-remaster-feel/)
- The toggle exists "to remind people that yes, this is the same game that's running underneath", inspired by StarCraft: Remastered *(excerpt)*. Sources differ on concept art: one says every asset was remade, another that in many cases the original concept art was reused instead of new concepts *(excerpt)*. — [Wikipedia D2R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diablo_II:_Resurrected); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/diablo-2s-lost-assets-werent-really-a-problem-for-diablo-2-resurrected/)

**Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary (Saber/343, 2011): an often-criticised case**
- Architecture: Saber's renderer runs on top of the original Halo engine, which still drives gameplay. Every object got a proxy so the original code stayed untouched. New high-res visuals drifted from the low-poly collision, so Saber built tools and intermediate geometry and sometimes swapped objects (a blocky rock became a Forerunner structure) (Iones, *Game Developer Magazine*, 2012, as cited). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo:_Combat_Evolved_Anniversary)
- The art brief: art director Ben Cammarano set four tenets, "heroic vistas, iconic imagery and characters, clean and vibrant aesthetics, and visceral action". Concept artists **painted over original screenshots** to show how lighting, effects and textures could change. Microsoft vetoed Saber moving the ringworld and planets. Fan feedback forced changes to Forerunner floors, the assault rifle and the Chief. The toggle began as a dev comparison tool and shipped as a feature. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo:_Combat_Evolved_Anniversary)
- Criticism: the remaster "drains a huge amount of character". Landscapes lose their "haunting, austere aesthetic". The starry night sky is replaced with Earth-like daylight. Haze washes out the ring. Models were brought "more in-line with Halo: Reach". The reviewer calls it "misguided" and contrasts it with Black Mesa, which keeps the original's character (Rick Lane). — [bit-tech, 17 Mar 2020](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/halo-combat-evolved-anniversary-pc-review/1/)
- GameSpot (Chris Watters, 2011): "the simplicity of the classic look feels more sinister and alien" for the Flood. — [Wikipedia (citing GameSpot)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo:_Combat_Evolved_Anniversary)

**Shadow of the Colossus (Bluepoint, 2018)**
- It was rebuilt on the original PS2 code with all-new graphics. Producer Randall Lowe and TD Peter Dalton discussed it on a Game Informer podcast (the page is only a teaser). — [Game Informer, 15 Dec 2017](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2017/12/15/how-bluepoint-rebuilt-shadow-of-the-colossus-for-ps4.aspx)
- Ueda sent Sony a list of requested changes but declined to name them publicly. Reports conflict on his involvement. — [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/articles/shadow-of-the-colossus-creator-sent-sony-list-of-c/1100-6451327/)
- Pre-release comparison: Ueda's games have "a very distinctive look, defined by their heavy use of fog and bloom". The effects were probably used to hide PS2 limits. Side by side, the remake is "lacking an ethereal quality" and staff were divided (Sammy Barker). — [Push Square, 31 Oct 2017](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2017/10/shadow_of_the_colossus_ps4_and_ps2_comparisons_show_stark_changes)
- GamesBeat review: where the world once felt like a dream, the remake gives it a "crisp, clean look" *(excerpt)*. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/shadow-of-the-colossus-remake-review-they-never-made-games-like-this/)

**System Shock (Nightdive, 2023)**
- Stephen Kick (CEO): a "ground-up remake" kept "as true to that as we can possibly keep it". Nightdive's philosophy: "the game you remembered playing, but not necessarily the game that you actually played." Original creators Warren Spector and Paul Neurath reviewed builds, which helped the team judge whether they had "gone too far or maybe not far enough". The article also gives Nightdive's terms: enhanced edition, remaster, remake. — [80.lv, 6 Sep 2022](https://80.lv/articles/system-shock-developer-nightdive-explains-the-difference-between-remake-and-remaster/)
- Larry Kuperman: the deliberately pixelated texture look was meant so "you are playing a classic game" *(excerpt)*. Original 1994 textures were used as stand-ins during early access *(excerpt)*. A 2016 statement said the team researched what gave Citadel Station its unique look *(excerpt)*. — [Epic Games Store interview](https://store.epicgames.com/news/system-shock-interview-release-date); [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/system-shock-remake-interview-return-to-the-stage-of-history)

**Age of Empires III: DE (World's Edge/Tantalus, 2020)**
- Art Director Han Randhawa: the team feared "changing the art too much", "worked relentlessly to preserve the legacy art", and steered back when visuals "veer drastically far from the 'look' that folks remember". Bloom was reduced so bright areas don't blow out, balanced against nostalgia. "Higher fidelity was a major key, but so was clear readability." Subtle colour hints were added to monochrome areas "without drastically changing what folks remember". The **display context** changed: the originals were played on CRTs with scanlines, and backlit panels change how the art reads. — [ageofempires.com, 16 Oct 2020](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-art-of-aoe3de/)
- David Giles (Tantalus): PBR, LUTs and lighting let artists "build on the existing assets" and move them closer to the *original concept art* (Craig Mullins' concepts were a key reference). Brad May (Zero One): the brief asked for units and buildings to look hand-crafted by a model-maker with "slightly exaggerated silhouettes". — [ageofempires.com](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-art-of-aoe3de/)
- AoE II: DE still runs on the original engine, with every graphic redrawn in 4K so it holds up at any zoom *(excerpt)*. — [PC Games](https://www.pcgames.de/Age-of-Empires-Definitive-Edition-Spiel-61068/Tests/Review-Zwischen-Tradition-und-Moderne-1250635/); [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/preview/2019/08/23/living-up-to-its-name)

**Resident Evil 2 (Capcom, 2019)** *(all excerpts; the PC Gamer making-of did not load)*
- Director Kazunori Kadoi: keep the feel of the original RPD while increasing scale and realism. The RPD was partly modelled on Osaka's Central Public Hall. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/the-making-of-resident-evil-2-remakes-raccoon-city-police-department)
- Art director Satoshi Takamatsu: keeping Leon's original "greenhorn" look mattered, with realism added to the gear (a military gear manufacturer was consulted). — [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=220820)
- The iconic lobby shot (vaulted ceiling, statue) was kept. Some areas were widened for gameplay, so the scale isn't 1:1. — [GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/originals/397507-resident-evil-2-remake-changes-whats-new-whats-different-whats-the-same)

**Mafia: Definitive Edition (Hangar 13, 2020)** *(excerpts; the mafiagame.com article now redirects)*
- Art Director Petr Motejzik: keep the "maximum number of elements" from the original, improved and developed further. The team started with new concept art and mood pieces to pin down the essence. The city is "practically a character". Haden Blackman: Lost Heaven was completely rebuilt "to provide more vistas and points of interest without losing any of the original game's landmarks". — [mafiagame.com (redirected)](https://mafiagame.com/news/mafia-definitive-edition-art); [USgamer](https://www.usgamer.net/articles/mafia-definitive-edition-remake-hangar-13-interview)

**Crash Bandicoot N. Sane Trilogy (Vicarious Visions, 2017)** *(excerpts)*
- It was built on the original level geometry so it plays as close as possible to the original. Missing animations, textures and music were recreated by cross-referencing video footage, concept art and fan feedback. — [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2016/12/03/crash-bandicoot-n-sane-trilogy-first-screens-in-depth-details/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_Bandicoot_N._Sane_Trilogy)

**Command & Conquer Remastered (Petroglyph/Lemon Sky, 2020)** *(excerpts)*
- HD rebuild with new models "keeping the original designs". A fan Community Council and former Westwood staff at Petroglyph were involved. — [Lemon Sky](https://www.lemonskystudios.com/?p=3047); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/fan-service-done-right-how-ea-worked-with-fans-to-create-command-and-conquer-remastered-collection)

**Skywind (fan remake of Morrowind in the Skyrim engine)**
- FAQ: every asset is built from scratch. Artists "revisit Morrowind and its concept art to explore the intent of the original designs" and reinterpret rather than copy. Landmarks stay "in roughly the same place" and the worldspace is the same size and shape. No mounts, to keep "the impression of a vast island". Original music is used "sparingly". — [Skywind FAQ](https://tesrskywind.com/faq/)
- Additions must merge seamlessly with the established world, "the same, but different" *(excerpt)*. 2026 status: 2D art "99% complete", 28 of 30 level kits done, and no generative AI *(excerpt)*. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/how-skywind-is-recreating-a-modern-classic); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim/skywind-update-2026-morrowind-renewal)

**Oblivion Remastered (Virtuos/Bethesda, April 2025)**
- Nicolas Roginski (design director, Virtuos), 2024 interview: "we aim at recreating the 'feeling of memory,' not the actual memory." — [Tech4Gamers, 22 Apr 2025](https://tech4gamers.com/elder-scrolls-oblivion-recreate-feelings-of-memory/)
- Palette shift: more magentas and oranges replace the original's heavy green. The reviewer still finds it beautiful but wishes for a 2006-colour option *(excerpt)*. Player threads complain of too much brown and a "generic UE5" look, and dislike the faces *(excerpts, opinion)*. Push Square: everything was remade in UE5 and not everyone will like the change in art direction *(excerpt)*. — [MobileSyrup, 11 May 2025](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/05/11/oblivion-remastered-captures-gaming-2006-with-style/); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2623190/discussions/0/604154270792661109); [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/previews/hands-on-oblivion-remastered-is-still-oblivion-in-all-its-glory-crap-frame-rate-aside)
- Gamereactor: the mood and essence are preserved, though characters can look too caricatured *(excerpt)*. — [Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/the-elder-scrolls-iv-oblivion-remastered-1534583/)

### Inferences
- The well-received projects share a split: **the logic layer is untouchable, the presentation layer is rebuilt.** D2R, Halo CEA and SotC all run original code underneath. Demon's Souls kept collision bounds. Crash kept level geometry. Fidelity rises inside a fixed frame.
- "Use the original's references, not your taste" shows up repeatedly: Bluepoint's original surface types, the AoE3 team moving assets "closer to the concept art", Skywind's concept-art study, D2R reusing original concepts. Detail is added where the old hardware made a design *ambiguous*, not where the team wanted something new.
- Halo CEA shows the failure can be written into the brief. "Clean and vibrant aesthetics" contradicted an original that critics loved for being austere, alien and night-lit. Painting over original screenshots does not help if the brief points the wrong way.
- "Remake the memory, not the reality" (Nightdive, Virtuos) is a double-edged rule. Nightdive anchored it with the original creators as a check. Oblivion's colour shift shows how "feeling of memory" can let a new team's taste stand in for the original palette.

### Gaps
- Final Fantasy VII Remake (Midgar densification) and the RE4 remake were not researched in this pass. I found no art-direction source for them.
- I found no official Bluepoint or Ueda statement on SotC's fog, bloom or colour-grading decisions. The Game Informer podcast was not transcribed.
- I could not open Digital Foundry primary analyses (SotC, Oblivion, GTA Trilogy). Only secondary coverage was used.
- The Mafia DE and RE2 quotes come from excerpts only. The original pages did not load.

## Q2. What is known about the Gothic 1 Remake's art direction and fan feedback on fidelity, and about Gothic texture packs?

### Takeaway
Alkimia publicly rejected an "HD version of the art style". It reinterpreted the look ("grounded, realistic" plus fantastical, a "Baroque" visual theory) while keeping the geography and camp structure. Critics after the 5 June 2026 release mostly found the geography and atmosphere faithful and the camps "things of beauty". Hardcore fans objected earlier to reinterpreted creatures and locations ("generic fantasy", "aztec temple"). The 2019 Playable Teaser is the clearest Gothic case of losing the soul: it was too bright, not gloomy, and didn't feel like a prison. Gothic II texture packs explicitly aim to stay close to vanilla, but I found little documented fan reaction.

### Cited Findings

**History and process**
- Dec 2019: THQ Nordic Barcelona released the *Gothic Playable Teaser* with a survey. It had over 180,000 downloads and over 30,000 survey responses, mostly mixed, with over 90% favouring a remake. Feb 2020: the remake was announced, the teaser scrapped, and a "complete reboot" approved based on feedback asking for something closer to the original. March 2021: Alkimia Interactive was founded in Barcelona. Original Gothic developers joined (composer Kai Rosenkranz, a dialogue writer, a programmer). All staff were required to play the original. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_1_Remake)
- Game Director Reinhard Pollice on the teaser: "We intentionally went in a direction we knew like 'OK, we don't think they will like this,'". It "worked out because we got a strong sentiment about what people liked about the original." — [DualShockers, 15 Sep 2023](https://www.dualshockers.com/gothic-remake-nothing-like-2019-teaser/)
- Teaser criticism (Dec 2019): "Everything is just literally too bright." The original had a "dull, muted, washed-out color palette", against the teaser's bluer sky, greener vegetation and bloom. The Colony "doesn't look gloomy or scary, and doesn't really feel like a prison". The barrier looks like a soap bubble rather than a menacing plasma field. The reviewer wanted a "mostly sunless sky overcast with gray clouds" and the original soundtrack. Turning off some post-processing brought it closer to the original. The map stayed close to the original, so world design was "much less of a concern". — [The Nocturnal Rambler, 22 Dec 2019](http://thenocturnalrambler.blogspot.com/2019/12/gothic-remake-playable-teaser-feedback.html)
- Similar Steam feedback on the teaser: too cheerful, should be darker and grimier, palette too bright *(excerpts)*. — [Steam thread 1](https://steamcommunity.com/app/950670/discussions/0/3145133021635977487/); [Steam thread 2](https://steamcommunity.com/app/950670/discussions/0/3145133021635564289/)

**Stated art direction**
- Pollice (Making Of: Art Design video): "We also decided very early on, we are not making an HD version of the art style of the original, that's not our goal." He and Studio Art Director Daniel Candil describe balancing a grounded, realistic look with the fantastical and magical elements. — [Wccftech, 8 Jul 2025](https://wccftech.com/alkimia-interactive-delves-into-its-approach-to-art-in-the-gothic-remake/)
- Candil at Gamelab (Málaga) described a "Baroque artistic approach", "a clear visual theory and artist agency". Candil is an art professor with a doctorate in fine arts, and the excerpt frames his approach as studying old masters and applying it to modern production *(partly excerpt)*. — [Gamereactor, 31 Oct 2024](https://www.gamereactor.eu/video/710333/An+exclusive+insight+into+the+Gothic+Remake+and+its+Baroque+artistic+approach+at+Gamelab+Nexus/)
- THQ Nordic making-of "Chapter 1: The Essence of Gothic" (Pollice, Rosenkranz, Candil): it "balances staying true to the original while enhancing the experience". It shows "iconic locations from the original game" and "the evolution of the world's atmosphere and detail" (no specifics on the page). — [THQ Nordic, 7 Feb 2025](https://thqnordic.com/news/behind-the-scenes-the-making-of-gothic-1-remake)
- Pollice (Kairos interview, Poznan Game Arena): "The mains goal is to simply stay true to the original". Orcs were redesigned because the originals "worked due to their low-polygon look" and scaled up would not read as real creatures, but the goal was not "typical over the top grim creatures". On canon: "We want every reference to make sense". Fans who object to small changes are pointed to Gothic Classic. The world is 20–25% larger. — [Kairos interview, 26 Oct 2024](https://www.gothic.wiki/w/Kairos_Interview_with_Reinhard_Pollice)
- Wikipedia gives the world as 10–30% larger. Armour and NPCs were designed by Gate21 (Sarajevo). NPC faces were built with deep learning and morphing to avoid the original's repeated faces. Rosenkranz reinterpreted his soundtrack and wrote new music. UE4 moved to UE5.4. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_1_Remake) (conflicts slightly with the Kairos 20–25% figure)

**Fan criticism before release (fidelity)**
- Steam thread, 11–13 Dec 2024: the game is called a reboot because of "artistic liberty" in re-imagining creatures. "goblins, orcs, demons now look too generic fantasy". One poster asks why the troll canyon became a "fantasy aztec temple": "A remake is about recreating, not re-interpreting." The Old and New Camps are called the only easily recognisable places. Others defend it: same story, locations and quest-givers, "Let them cook". — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1297900/discussions/0/561358128180889646)
- The Feuerwaran (fire lizard) redesign looks more like a small dragon than the original's monitor-lizard body. Fans felt designers "went a bit too far" and asked for the original head shape *(excerpt; page 403)*. — [GameStar](https://www.gamestar.de/artikel/gothic-remake-feuerwaran,3407868.html)
- A fan-wiki overview reports debate about changed skin tones (e.g. the Old Camp castle gate guards, Cavalorn). It also reports that later trailers moved to a darker, more desaturated style and back toward the original character designs *(excerpt; low-reliability source)*. — [shapes.inc fandom](https://shapes.inc/fandom/gothic-1-remake/controversies)
- A pre-release Steam commenter wanted more contrast and a grittier colony, "a gloomy prison camp" *(excerpt, opinion)*. — [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1297900/discussions/0/600765465840621639)

**Reception after release (release 5 June 2026)**
- Metacritic 74 (PC) and 69 (PS5). OpenCritic 57% recommend. GameStar 83, PC Games 8/10, IGN 7/10, PC Gamer 60. PC Games praised a "Gothic feeling". GameStar said the classic feel returned. 4Players gave 9/10 but viewed the faithfulness negatively. Eurogamer.de criticised issues carried over from the original. IGN Germany praised visuals and faithfulness. 500,000 units sold in the first week. Nominated for Golden Joystick Best Remake/Remaster 2026. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_1_Remake)
- Screen Hype (Luigi Savinelli): "astonishingly faithful in its geography". The trail to the Old Camp, the Exchange Place and the New Camp lake tavern are recognisable at a glance. Additions "blend in so naturally". The Old Camp fortress is a major upgrade over the blocky original. The Valley "has never looked this atmospheric". Camps feel lived-in. It "looks good, but not that good" for its performance cost. — [Screen Hype, 12 Jun 2026](https://www.screenhype.co.uk/gothic-1-remake-review-old-camp-new-tricks/)
- RPG Site (Bryan Vitale): environmental design and lighting were "a highlight". It was faithful from the first hour: the Old Camp entrance, the path to Cavalorn's cabin, and Mud and Fisk in their original places. Alkimia "showed restraint" by not inventing new plotlines. Many interiors are very dark without a torch or light spell. Animations and lip sync are dated. — [RPG Site, 18 Jun 2026](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/20727-gothic-1-remake-review)
- Other reviews: the camps are "things of beauty" that feel new with the same structure. The remake retains the atmosphere and "doubles down" on it. The world is "just as brutal, though much prettier" *(excerpts)*. — [Summit Reviews](https://summitreviews.biz/article/122/gothic-1-remake-review); [VideoGameLizard](https://videogamelizard.com/reviews/gothic-1-remake-review/); [PSU](https://www.psu.com/reviews/gothic-1-remake-ps5-review/)

**Gothic II texture packs**
- Vurt's G2 / Chronicles of Myrtana Graphics Overhaul (June 2018): 600+ new textures, 60+ new or improved models, new rain, sun, skies, trees and water, higher-poly rocks, no gameplay changes. — [DSOGaming, 15 Jun 2018](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/this-gothic-2-graphics-mod-adds-600-new-textures-and-60-new-or-improved-models/)
- Vurt's own description: the textures are "resynthesized, thus they are entirely new", but he tried to keep them similar to the original in style *(excerpt; Nexus 403)*. — [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/gothic2/mods/91)
- Merlin's Texturepack "tries to stay as close to the original as possible" (single VDF, about 688 MB) *(excerpt; ModDB 403)*. The GOTHIC 2 HD TEXTURES PACK is a 2.26 GB compilation. ARTstuff's vegetation compilation bundles Merlin's and Vurt's work and warns of conflicts *(excerpts)*. ### Inferences

## Q3. What practical rules emerge?

### Takeaway
These rules recur across studios: lock layout, collision, proportions and silhouettes. Keep information parity and darkness. Use the original's own references (concept art, surface types, original code and timing) as the target. Add detail only where hardware made the original ambiguous. Verify with side-by-side toggles or screenshot comparisons. Make feel-changing changes optional. Use original creators or a fan council to say "too far / not far enough". Account for how the original was displayed. Write the brief from the original's adjectives, not generic modern ones.

### Cited Findings
- **Lock the structure.** Layout, encounters and creatures are not adjusted, and collision bounds are not altered (Bluepoint). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake). Build on the original level geometry (Crash *(excerpt)*). — [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2016/12/03/crash-bandicoot-n-sane-trilogy-first-screens-in-depth-details/). Keep landmarks while rebuilding the city (Mafia DE *(excerpt)*). — [USgamer](https://www.usgamer.net/articles/mafia-definitive-edition-remake-hangar-13-interview). Keep landmarks "in roughly the same place" (Skywind). — [Skywind FAQ](https://tesrskywind.com/faq/)
- **Keep proportions and silhouettes.** Character proportions stay close to the originals (Bluepoint). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake). Same silhouettes and visibility in both modes (D2R). — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/). Readability matters as much as fidelity (AoE3 DE). — [ageofempires.com](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-art-of-aoe3de/)
- **Information parity and darkness as design.** Never show more or less information. Lighting must not reveal what should stay unseen, and darkness is part of the experience (D2R). — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- **Upgrade where the old hardware was the limit.** Add detail to designs that were ambiguous because of polygons and textures, and bring in concept-art elements the hardware couldn't show (Bluepoint). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake). Move assets closer to the original concept art (AoE3 DE). — [ageofempires.com](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-art-of-aoe3de/). The counter-case: fog was both a hardware mask and mood, and removing it exposed play-area edges and killed the mystery (Silent Hill HD). — [DualShockers, 24 Mar 2012](https://www.dualshockers.com/former-silent-hill-art-director-comes-down-on-hd-collection/)
- **Material vocabulary from the original.** Reuse the original surface types (cobblestone, mossy brick) when planning materials (Bluepoint). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- **Quantified fidelity budget.** Keep at least 70% of the original look and colours, the "70/30 philosophy" (D2R). — [Blizzard Watch](https://blizzardwatch.com/2021/02/26/diablo-2-resurrected-remaster-feel/)
- **Toggle and side-by-side as a QA tool.** D2R QA compared screenshots of what is visible per mode, and the toggle exposes pixel-level deviations. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/). Halo CEA's toggle started as a dev comparison tool. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo:_Combat_Evolved_Anniversary). Demon's Souls added a "classic" filter in photo mode. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon%27s_Souls_(2020_video_game))
- **Paint over original screenshots** to explore upgrades (Halo CEA concept artists). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halo:_Combat_Evolved_Anniversary)
- **Keep the original timing.** Decouple render framerate per animation so motion is smoother with the same timing, and remake cinematics frame for frame (D2R). — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/)
- **Optional rather than imposed.** Changes that affect feel become toggles, and quirks follow the rule "what players remember most" (D2R). — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2021/09/21/behind-diablo-ii-resurrected-authenticity-developer-interview/). Fans who want zero changes are pointed to the original (Gothic Classic, Pollice). — [Kairos interview](https://www.gothic.wiki/w/Kairos_Interview_with_Reinhard_Pollice)
- **External "too far / not far enough" check.** Original creators reviewed builds (System Shock). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/system-shock-developer-nightdive-explains-the-difference-between-remake-and-remaster/). Community Council plus former Westwood staff (C&C Remastered *(excerpt)*). — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/fan-service-done-right-how-ea-worked-with-fans-to-create-command-and-conquer-remastered-collection). Original developers on the team, and all staff had to play the original (Gothic Remake). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_1_Remake)
- **Account for display context.** The art was made for CRTs with scanlines, so on backlit panels bloom was reduced to avoid blow-out (AoE3 DE). — [ageofempires.com](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-art-of-aoe3de/)
- **Set tone at composition level first**, then build modular kits tiered by narrative progression (Bluepoint). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/demon-s-souls-how-to-make-a-great-remake)
- **Provoke on purpose to learn what matters.** The Gothic teaser was deliberately aimed where the team expected disapproval, to surface "what people liked about the original". — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/gothic-remake-nothing-like-2019-teaser/)
- **Texture-replacer rule.** Match colour, saturation and brightness to the replaced vanilla texture (Morrowind VanillaPlus's stated aim, *excerpt, exact page not pinned*). Users fault Intelligent Textures for colour drift despite its higher quality. — [Nexus comments](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/47469?tab=posts)

### Inferences
- "Ask why before changing" is not stated in those words by any source I found. It is implied by Bluepoint's "studied the original developer's intent" step and by D2R's case-by-case bug rule. The operational form is: for each feature to be changed, classify it as intent, technical limit, or both (fog is both).
- When a feature is both a limit and part of the mood (fog, darkness, low-poly menace), the safe move seen in praised work is to **keep the mood effect and remove only the artefact**: keep darkness but light dropped items (D2R), keep the fog density but at PS2 resolution and speed (the Silent Hill 2 fan Enhanced Edition, below).
- For a "same Gothic, better made" programme, the evidence suggests a concrete checklist: same layout and collision; same proportions and silhouettes; same palette, value and brightness per surface; same darkness and visibility; detail added only to resolve ambiguity; side-by-side captures at the same spots and times of day; any deviation needs a stated reason.

### Gaps
- I found no published "intent document" template from any studio. Bluepoint, Vicarious Visions and Alkimia describe the practice but publish no format.
- I found no GDC postmortem in this pass that gives a formal side-by-side comparison policy. The evidence is from interviews.

## Q4. Fan criticism patterns: which changes do fans perceive as losing the soul?

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are: lost fog, haze or darkness, and so lost mystery. A palette or grade drift toward generic modern looks (bright, saturated, brown, "generic UE5"). A changed sky, time of day or lighting that turns alien or oppressive places homely. Creature and character designs pulled toward "generic fantasy". Faces. Sanitised or "too pretty" places that should feel wretched (a prison colony). An over-clean "crisp" look replacing a dreamy or austere one. Fans tolerate, and often praise, added detail when structure, value and mood hold.

### Cited Findings
- **Removing fog or haze**: Silent Hill HD Collection removed fog, exposing where the play area ends and weakening the mystery. Former art director Masahiro Ito: "It's poor. It's really a released version? Really?" — [DualShockers, 24 Mar 2012](https://www.dualshockers.com/former-silent-hill-art-director-comes-down-on-hd-collection/). Fog also masked hardware limits, and the remaster removed that cover *(excerpt)*. — [WhatCulture](https://whatculture.com/gaming/10-video-game-remasters-that-removed-things-fans-loved?page=5). Fans later restored PS2-quality fog, including the original fog texture resolution and movement speed, in the Silent Hill 2 Enhanced Edition *(excerpt)*. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/updated-silent-hill-2-fan-patch-upscales-images-improves-audio-fixes-bugs-and-more); [Enhanced Edition site](https://silenthillmemories.net/sh2/enhanced_edition_en.htm)
- **GTA Trilogy Definitive Edition**: fog was removed at launch and later made re-enableable. AI-upscaled and misaligned textures, lighting heavily criticised, a classic-sky option restored in a later update *(excerpts)*. — [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/gta-trilogy-definitive-edition-changes-differences-content); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-the-trilogy-definitive-edition-patches-1848920282); [LevelUp, Nov 2024](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/after-3-years-gta-the-trilogy-the-definitive-edition-receives-update-and-improves-one-of-its-most-criticized-aspects-rockstar-has-already-optimized-it)
- **Too clean or crisp in place of dreamy**: SotC remake is "lacking an ethereal quality". — [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2017/10/shadow_of_the_colossus_ps4_and_ps2_comparisons_show_stark_changes). ### Inferences
- Nearly every soul-loss complaint is about **value and atmosphere (fog, darkness, sky, grade)** or **identity of designs (creatures, faces)**. Few are about resolution or density of detail. Fans accept more detail; they reject a different *mood* or a different *shape*.
- "Sanitised poverty" did not appear as a named critique in the sources I found. The nearest equivalents are the teaser's "doesn't feel like a prison" and the pre-release "gloomy prison camp" posts about the Gothic colony.
- Toggles and fan restorations (Halo classic mode, the GTA fog and sky options, the Silent Hill 2 Enhanced Edition) work as a public audit of whether the remaster read the style correctly. One user review of Halo CEA, seen only as a search excerpt and not attributed reliably, says its author played almost entirely in classic mode for this reason.

### Gaps
- I found no source naming "sanitised poverty" or "over-cleaned grime" as a critique of any specific remake. This needs targeted search, e.g. RE4 village or Mafia DE slums, which I did not cover.
- I found no Warcraft III: Reforged or FF VII Remake art-direction criticism in this pass.
- Many fan-sentiment items are single forum posts or user reviews. They show what fans say but not how widely the view is held.
