Character concept turnaround sheet: the Nameless Hero, the main character of the video game Gothic II (Piranha Bytes, 2002), as he looks in Gothic II, remade faithfully with modern graphics. He must be recognised at first glance by anyone who played the game: the same face, the same hair, the same goatee, not a new design.

The head, neck and top of the shoulders, shown four times side by side on a plain mid-grey background (#7a7c80): front view, three-quarter view (facing left), left profile, and back of the head. Same man, same calm expression, same scale and eye height in all four views. Soft even studio light from the upper front-left, no rim light. Rendered like a high-end realistic 3D game character sculpt (real skin, real hair strands), not a painting and not a photo of a real person. Wide landscape image, about 3:1.

Clothing only as a hint: the collar of a coarse undyed linen shirt. No armour, no scars, no tattoos, no earrings.
